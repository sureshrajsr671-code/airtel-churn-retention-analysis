# Airtel Customer Churn Diagnostics & Retention Strategy

Uncovering Subscriber Defection Patterns, Revenue Risks, and Strategic Retention Roadmaps for Airtel.

---

## 📌 Executive Diagnostics & Overview
Is empirical study me Airtel ke **7,043 active subscriber accounts** ka granular data analysis kiya gaya hai, jisse market share retain kiya ja sake aur voluntary customer attrition ko roka ja sake[cite: 8].

### Key Performance Metrics:
- **Total Analyzed Subscribers:** 7,043 accounts[cite: 8]
- **Total Churned Subscribers:** 1,869 customers (Overall Churn Rate: **26.5%**)[cite: 8]
- **Monthly Revenue Leakage:** ~$1.40M+ in lost Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)[cite: 8]
- **Customer Acquisition Multiplier:** Replacement cost **5x to 7x** zyada hai retention ke mukable, jisse churn mitigation Airtel ki highest-ROI initiative ban jati hai[cite: 8].

## 📊 Dataset & Analytics Scope
- **Customer Demographic & Account Records:** 7,043 profiles covering tenure, senior citizen status, and partner billing[cite: 8].
- **Account & Billing Attributes:** Contract terms (Month-to-month, 1-Year, 2-Year), payment gateways, and monthly charges ($)[cite: 8].
- **Service Stack Tracking:** Fiber Optic, DSL broadband, Online Security, Tech Support, and device protection add-ons[cite: 8].

---

## 🔍 Core Behavioral Churn Catalysts

### 1. Contract & Payment Friction
- **Month-to-Month Contracts:** In subscribers ka churn rate **42.7%** hai, kyunki koi commitment lock-in na hone se rival promo offers me migrate hona aasan ho jata hai[cite: 8].
- **Electronic Check Payments:** Electronic check se pay karne wale subscribers me **45.2% churn** darj hua hai, jo billing friction aur manual renewal delays ko darshata hai[cite: 8].

### 2. Fiber Optic Price-Value Disconnect
- Fiber Optic users ka churn rate **41.9%** hai (jabki DSL users ka sirf **19.0%**)[cite: 8].
- Premium bill brackets ($70–$110/month) ke bawajood jab technical assistance ya device protection missing hoti hai, to customer value mismatch me cancel kar deta hai[cite: 8].

### 3. Early Tenure Vulnerability & Missing Support
- **First-Year Attrition:** 50%+ customer attrition shuruati **12 months** ke andar hi hoti hai[cite: 8].
- **Protection Add-on Impact:** Jin users ke paas Online Security ya Tech Support nahi hai, wo protected users ke mukable **3x rate (41% vs 14%)** par churn karte hain[cite: 8].
- Unassisted technical glitches face karne wale customers defect hone se pehle customer care me 3 baar tak reach out karte hain[cite: 8].

| Customer Segment Profile | Churn Rate | Avg Monthly Bill | Primary Risk Driver | Risk Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

> **Key Takeaway:** Month-to-Month Electronic Check users ko 1-Year ya 2-Year Auto-Pay contracts me convert karne se churn risk **80% se zyada reduce** ho jata hai[cite: 8].

## 🛠️ Strategic Retention Roadmap & Interventions

- **Q1 - Early Onboarding (Tenure Protection):** Naye subscribers ke liye structured 90-day onboarding nudges, active network health checks aur welcome discounts roll out karna[cite: 8].
- **Q2 - Auto-Pay Incentives (Payment Friction Elimination):** Electronic Check users ko Bank ACH / Auto-Pay me switch karne par $5/month ka direct bill credit provide karna[cite: 8].
- **Q3 - Value Bundles (Fiber Optic Protection):** High-ARPU Fiber Optic plans ke saath complimentary Norton Online Security aur 24/7 Priority Tech Support bundle karna[cite: 8].
- **Q4 - Predictive Machine Learning Engine:** Customer service desk par real-time ML churn scoring engine integrate karna taaki risk alert hone par customer care calls ke dauran proactive retention offers deploy ho sakein[cite: 8].
